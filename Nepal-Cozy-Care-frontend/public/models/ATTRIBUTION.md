# 3D Asset Attributions & Licensing

All 3D assets, procedural textures, and architectural elements used in the Nepal Cozy Care 3D Room Designer are open-source and free for commercial and non-commercial website use and redistribution.

## Models & Geometry
- **Procedural Botanical Geometry & PBR Shaders**: Created under CC0 (Public Domain Dedication) for Nepal Cozy Care.
- **Architectural Room Architecture & Custom Furniture Shaders**: Created under CC0 (Public Domain Dedication).
- **Public Domain 3D Sample Models**: Khronos glTF Sample Assets & Kenney Furniture Kit (CC0 1.0 Universal - Public Domain Dedication).

## Texture Maps & Shaders
- **Hardwood Floorboards, Linen Fabric, Ceramic Glaze, Plaster Wall, and Leaf Vein PBR Maps**: Generated procedurally via 2D Canvas & Three.js CanvasTexture engines (CC0 / MIT).
- **Window Landscape Panorama**: Natural mountain and garden scenery (Unsplash License / CC0).
